Seconda media

Regola 31

Was e were: il passato di to be

Le parole

  • was [uòz]
  • were [uöö]
  • wasn’t [uòzent]
  • born [bòon]

Le frasi

  • I was born in Milan.
    Sono nato a Milano.
  • Where were you yesterday?
    Dov’eri ieri?
  • There was a party at Tom’s house.
    C’era una festa a casa di Tom.
  • The shops weren’t open.
    I negozi non erano aperti.

L’audio legge prima le parole, con una pausa per ripetere, e poi le frasi, due volte ciascuna.
